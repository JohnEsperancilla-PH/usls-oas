# OASYS — Step-by-Step Guides

Simple, point-by-point instructions for the three main tasks.

- **Guide 1** — Booking an Appointment (End User / Visitor)
- **Guide 2** — Using the Office Dashboard (SOA / Office Head)
- **Guide 3** — Using the Gate / Entry Page (Campus Security)

---

# Guide 1 — Booking an Appointment (End User)

**Where:** https://oasys.usls.edu.ph
**No account or login needed.**

## Step 1 — Start
1. Open the website.
2. Read the welcome popup, then click **Book an Appointment**.

## Step 2 — Your Details (Step 1 of 2)
Fill in the form:
1. **Full Name**
2. **Phone Number**
3. **Email Address** — this is where your reference number will be sent
4. **Visitor Category** — pick one: External / Parents / Alumni / Vendor
5. **Valid ID** — choose the ID you will bring to the guard
6. **Number of Visitors** — leave as `1 Visitor`, or click **Multiple Visitors** if coming in a group (max 10)
7. **Vehicles** — only if you are bringing a vehicle (max 5)
8. **Tick the consent checkbox**
9. Click **Next: Appointment Details**

## Step 3 — Appointment Details (Step 2 of 2)
1. **Select Office** — search or click a department, then pick your office
2. **Person to Meet** — choose from the list, or pick **Others…** and type a name
3. **Pick a Date** — click a date on the calendar
   - Greyed-out dates are unavailable (weekends, past, or too far ahead)
4. **Pick a Time** — click an available time slot
   - `Full` = no slots left · `Blocked` = office closed · `Passed` = time is over
5. **Visit Duration** — pick **30** or **60** minutes
6. **Purpose of Visit** — type why you are visiting
7. Check the **Booking Summary**
8. Click **Submit Appointment**

## Step 4 — Wait for the Email
- You get a **confirmation email** right away — status is **Pending**.
- Then the office reviews it. You will receive one of these:

| Email | What it means |
|---|---|
| ✅ **Approved** | Contains your **reference number** — you can now visit |
| ⏳ **Postponed** | Moved to a new date/time |
| ❌ **Declined** | Not approved — you can book again |

> ⚠️ **Save the reference number.** It is your gate pass.

## On Your Visit Day
- Arrive **20–30 minutes early**.
- Bring your **reference number** and the **valid ID** you selected.
- Entry is allowed **30 minutes before** up to **1 hour after** your time.

# Guide 2 — Using the Office Dashboard (SOA / Office Head)

**Where:** https://oasys.usls.edu.ph/admin/login
**Login:** the email and password given to you.

> 👀 You only see and manage **your own office**.

## A. Signing In
1. Go to the admin login page.
2. Enter your **email** and **password**.
3. Click **Sign In**.

## B. Understanding the Dashboard
- **Top cards** — counts for Pending, Approved, Declined, Total
- **Filter buttons** — `Pending`, `Approved`, `Postponed`, `Declined`, `History`, `All`
- **Calendar / List toggle** — switch between a calendar and a list view
- **+ New Invitation** — create a pre-approved pass (see section G)
- Each row shows the visitor, date/time, and status — with **View**, **Approve**, and **Decline** buttons

---

## C. Approve an Appointment

**From the dashboard:**
1. Find the booking (use the **Pending** filter).
2. Click **Approve** — or click **View** to open it first.
3. Confirm.

**Or open the details (View) for the full options:**
1. Click **View** on the booking.
2. Read the visitor details.
3. Click **Approve**.

> ✅ The visitor gets an **approval email** with their **reference number**, and a calendar event is created.
> - If asked for an **email** for the person to meet, type it — they get notified.
> - If asked for a **time**, pick one — some offices don't let visitors choose.

## D. Decline an Appointment
1. Click **View** on the booking (or **Decline** from the row).
2. Click **Decline**.
3. (Optional) type a **reason**.
4. Click **Confirm Decline**.

> ❌ The visitor gets a decline email with a link to rebook.

## E. Postpone an Appointment
1. Click **View** on the booking.
2. Click **Postpone**.
3. Pick a **New Date** and **New Time**.
4. (Optional) type a **reason**.
5. Click **Confirm Postpone**.

> ⏳ The visitor gets a postponement email showing the old and new schedule.

## F. Review Emails Faster (No Login Needed)
1. Open the **alert email** for a new booking.
2. Click **Approve** or **Deny** directly in the email.

> ⏳ These links work for **72 hours**.

---

## G. Create an Invitation (Pre-Approved Pass)
Use this for a visitor you already cleared — skips the approval step.

1. Click **+ New Invitation**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Office**, **Date**, **Time**, **Duration**
   - **Visitor Full Name** (required)
   - Email, Phone, Category, Valid ID, Person to Meet, Purpose (optional)
3. Optional extras:
   - **Number of Visitors** (group)
   - **Vehicles** (plate numbers)
   - **Tag Office Contacts** — they get a calendar invite
4. Click **Create / Save**.

> 🎟️ The visitor is **approved immediately** and gets an email with a **PDF ticket**.
> 💡 To re-download a ticket later: **Invitations** list → **Ticket PDF**.

---

## H. Block Office Schedule (Close a Time Slot)
1. Go to the **Calendar** view.
2. **Hover over a date** and click the **+** button — or click **Block slots** in list view.
3. In the **Block Time Slots** window, click a time to **block** it.
4. To reopen it, click the blocked (red) slot again to **unblock**.

> 🚫 Blocked slots no longer appear as available to visitors.

## I. Add Office Contacts
Contacts can be tagged on invitations so they get notified.

1. Go to **Contacts** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Add Contact**.
3. Enter **Name**, **Email**, and **Position**.
4. Click **Add Contact** to save.

**To change one:** click **Edit** → update → **Update Contact**
**To delete one:** click **Remove** → confirm

> ℹ️ Super admins manage contacts from **Offices** → the **Contacts** button on an office.

# Guide 3 — Using the Gate / Entry Page (Campus Security)

**Where:** https://oasys.usls.edu.ph/entry
**Login:** your **Employee ID or Username** + password.

## A. Logging In
1. Open the entry page on the gate device.
2. Type your **Employee ID or Username**.
3. Type your **password**.
4. Click **Sign In**.

## B. Signing Out
- Click **Sign Out** (top right) at the end of your shift.

## C. Know the Screen
- **Left — Today's expected visitors:** the list of approved visitors for today. Click a name to auto-fill their reference.
- **Right — Gate Entry:** the camera area with **Start Camera** and a manual-entry button.

---

## D. Enable the Camera to Scan
1. Click **Start Camera**.
2. **Allow camera permission** if the browser asks.
3. Point the camera at the visitor's **QR code**.

> ⚠️ Camera needs a secure (**HTTPS**) connection. If it won't start, use **manual entry** (see below).

## E. Get the Visitor's Code
You can do this **either** way:

- ✅ **Scan the QR code** from the visitor's email or ticket
- 🔢 **Enter the Unique Code manually** — click the **keypad button**, type the reference number, then click **Verify Reference Number**
- 📋 **Or click the visitor's name** in *Today's expected visitors* — this auto-fills the code

## F. Review the Details
A preview appears showing:
- Visitor name, email, phone
- Office and person to meet
- Date, time, and duration
- **Valid ID to Present** and the reference number
- Registered vehicles and group visitors (if any)

**Now check the visitor's actual ID matches what's on screen.**

## G. Approve or Deny Entry

**To let them in:**
1. Click **Allow Entry**.
2. Entry is recorded automatically (status becomes *Completed / Checked in*).

**To refuse entry:**
1. Click **Deny Entry**.
2. Type a **reason**.
3. Click **Confirm Denial**.

## H. Record Entry and Exit
- **Entry** is recorded automatically when you click **Allow Entry** — the time is shown on screen.
- **Exit** — when the visitor leaves, verify their code again, then click **Record Visitor Checkout**.

## I. Common Messages

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Too early / too late** | Outside the entry window (30 min before → 1 hr after). Wait or reject. |
| **Already used** | Code was already scanned — **do not** let them in. |
| **Wrong date / expired** | Not their visit day — do not admit. |
| **Pending** | Not yet approved — the office must approve it first. |
| **Not found** | Code is wrong — type it again; if it still fails, contact the office. |
| **Denied** | The office said no — do not admit. |

---

## J. If the Power or Internet Goes Out
Use the **paper log book**. Write down:
1. Reference / unique code
2. Visitor name
3. Time
4. ID shown
5. Office

Admit them based on the paper, then reconcile with the system when it's back.

## K. Device Care
- **Charge** the device at the end of every shift.
- **Keep it at the post** — never take it home.
- **Report damage or loss** to your supervisor right away.
- **Sign out** before handing it to the next guard.
