# OASYS Systems Integration
## Online Appointment System

**Training Guide**

University of St. La Salle

---

## About This Training

The systems integration of the **Online Appointment System (OASYS)** will follow the provided integration procedures and steps.

This ensures that the designated departments and offices are correctly oriented on:
- How the system works
- How their office or department will use the system

**Who this training is for:**
- 👔 Senior Office Associates (SOAs) and Office Heads
- 🛡️ Campus Security Personnel

# Part 1
## Senior Office Associates (SOAs) and Office Heads

---

## Who Are the SOAs and Office Heads?

The **SOAs and Office Heads** are the main users of this system.

They are expected to:
- ✅ **Approve** appointments
- ❌ **Decline** appointments
- ⏳ **Postpone** appointments
- 🏢 **Manage** office appointments and operations

> 🔑 Everything in OASYS starts with them.

---

## Training Outline

1. SOA and Office Head account creation
2. Finalization of Office Details
3. Complete Run Through of the System
   - Booking Simulation (End User)
   - Admin Side Invitations
   - Approving, Declining, & Postponing
   - Blocking Off of Office Schedules
   - Adding Office Contacts

---

# 1. Account Creation

- The **Super Admin** creates your account
- You receive your **email** and **password**
- Go to **oasys.usls.edu.ph/admin/login**
- Sign in with your email and password

> 📌 Each account is tied to **one office**. You only see and manage your own office.

---

# 2. Finalization of Office Details

Before booking opens, confirm your office setup:

- 🏢 Office **name** and department
- 📞 Office **contact** email and phone
- 🕐 **Operating hours**
- 👥 **Capacity** per time slot

> The Super Admin sets these up. Report any errors right away.

---

# 3. Complete Run Through of the System

Now let's walk through every task you'll do.

**In this section:**
- Booking Simulation
- Admin Side Invitations
- Approving, Declining, & Postponing
- Blocking Off of Office Schedules
- Adding Office Contacts

---

## 3.1 Booking Simulation — End User

See what a visitor sees. Book a test appointment:

1. Open **oasys.usls.edu.ph** (visitor view)
2. Click **Book an Appointment**
3. Fill in name, phone, email, visitor type, valid ID
4. Pick your **office**, person to meet, and purpose
5. Pick a **date**, **time**, and **duration** (30/60 min)
6. Submit

> ✅ A **pending** booking appears on your dashboard — and you get an alert email.

---

## 3.2 Admin Side Invitations

For visitors you already know are coming — no approval needed:

1. Go to **Invitations** → **New Invitation**
2. Fill in visitor details, office, date, and time
3. Optionally add group members, vehicles, and contacts
4. Submit

> 🎟️ The visitor is **immediately approved** and gets an email with a **PDF ticket**.

---

## 3.3 Approving, Declining, & Postponing

You can do this in **two ways** — from the dashboard, or straight from email.

---

## Through the Admin Dashboard

1. Open a booking from your **Appointments** list
2. Choose one:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| ✅ **Approve** | Says yes — gives the visitor a reference number |
| ❌ **Decline** | Says no — add a reason (optional) |
| ⏳ **Postpone** | Moves it to a new date and time |

> Approving creates a calendar event and emails the visitor.

---

## Through Email Notifications

You don't even need to log in:

1. Open the **alert email** for a new booking
2. Click **Approve** or **Deny** right in the email
3. The visitor is notified right away

> ⏳ These email links work for **72 hours**.

---

## 3.4 Blocking Off of Office Schedules

Need the office closed at a certain time?

1. Open the **calendar view**
2. Click the time slot you want to close
3. Click **Block** and add a reason

> Blocked slots **won't show as available** to visitors.

---

## 3.5 Adding Office Contacts

Keep a list of people you invite often:

1. Go to **Contacts**
2. Click **Add Contact**
3. Enter **name**, **email**, and **position**
4. Use **Edit** or **Remove** to manage the list

> Tag these contacts on invitations so they get the calendar invite and notification.

# Part 2
## Campus Security Personnel

---

## Who Are the Campus Security Personnel?

The **Campus Security Personnel** are assigned in rotation at **Gate 2** — the main entrance where the Visitor's Pass is generated and given to visitors.

**Your role in the system:**
- 🔍 **Scan** the QR code or code provided by the visitor
- 📋 **Review** the details shown
- 🚪 **Grant or deny** the visitor access to the campus

---

## Training Outline

1. Campus Security Personnel account creation
2. Complete Run Through of the System
   - Operating of the Mobile Device
   - Logging in and Signing out
   - Enabling the Camera to Scan
   - Input of provided details (QR Code or Unique Code)
   - How to record entry and exit
   - Device charging and safekeeping

---

# 1. Account Creation

- The **Super Admin** creates your account
- You receive your **Employee ID** (or username) and **password**
- Your account only gives access to the **gate station**

> 📌 Each guard has their own login. Never share it.

---

# 2. Complete Run Through of the System

**In this section:**
- Operating of the Mobile Device
- Logging in and Signing out
- Enabling the Camera to Scan
- Input of provided details (QR Code or Unique Code)
- How to record entry and exit
- Device charging and safekeeping

---

## Operating of the Mobile Device

- The gate uses a **mobile device** set up for OASYS
- Keep it **charged** and **secured** at the post
- Open the gate page: **oasys.usls.edu.ph/entry**
- Use it landscape for a bigger view

> 📱 Only use the device for the gate system.

---

## Logging In and Signing Out

**To log in:**
1. Open **oasys.usls.edu.ph/entry**
2. Type your **Employee ID or Username**
3. Type your **password**
4. Click **Sign In**

**To sign out:**
- Click **Sign Out** at the end of your shift

> ⚠️ Always sign out before you leave your post.

---

## Enabling the Camera to Scan

1. On the **Gate Entry** panel, tap **Start Camera**
2. Allow **camera permission** if asked
3. The camera preview appears

> ⚠️ The camera needs a **secure (HTTPS)** connection. If it won't start, use **manual entry** instead.

---

## Input of Provided Details

The visitor gives you **one** of two things. Both work:

- ✅ **QR Code** — scan it with the camera
- 🔢 **Unique Code** — type it in manually

After scanning or typing, a **preview** shows the visitor's details, office, time, and expected ID.

---

## Approve or Deny Entry

Check the details and the visitor's **valid ID**, then choose:

- ✅ **Allow Entry** — lets the visitor in
- ❌ **Deny Entry** — does not let them in (add a reason)

> Always confirm the **ID matches** the booking before allowing entry.

---

## How to Record Entry and Exit

- **Entry** is recorded the moment you tap **Allow Entry**
- **Exit** — when the visitor leaves, tap **Record Visitor Checkout**

> Both the entry and exit times are saved for the record.

---

## If Something Looks Wrong

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Too early / too late** | Outside the entry window |
| **Already used** | This code was already scanned — don't let them in |
| **Wrong date** | Not their visit day |
| **Pending** | Not approved yet |
| **Not found** | Code is wrong — type it again |
| **Denied** | The office said no — do not admit |

---

## If the Power or Internet Goes Out

Use the **paper log book**. Write down:

- Reference / unique code
- Visitor name
- Time
- ID shown
- Office

Let them in based on the paper. Reconcile in the system later.

---

## Device Charging and Safekeeping

- **Charge** the device at the end of every shift
- **Keep it at the post** — never take it home
- **Report damage or loss** to your supervisor right away
- **Sign out** before handing it to the next guard

> 🔋 A charged, secured device keeps the gate running.
