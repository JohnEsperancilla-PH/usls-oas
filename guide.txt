====================================================
USLS Online Appointment System (OAS)
USER MANUAL
====================================================

Welcome. This manual explains the Online Appointment System used by
the University of St. La Salle to manage visits to campus offices.

It is written for two kinds of people, and has three parts:

  Part A - For Visitors: how to book a visit, what happens next,
           how your details are used, and how to get through the gate.

  Part B - For Staff: how to review, approve, and decline requests,
           run the office calendar, and manage offices, accounts,
           and system options.

  Part C - Diving Deeper: a closer look for the curious -- what each
           piece of information you collect is actually used for, and
           how the system works under the hood.

Everything is written in plain language. No technical background is
needed to read Parts A and B. Part C goes a little further, but each
section there can still be read on its own.

PART A - FOR VISITORS
====================================================


----------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT THE SYSTEM DOES FOR YOU
----------------------------------------------------

The Online Appointment System lets you book a visit to a university
office ahead of time. No waiting in line, no walking in and hoping
for the best -- you choose when you want to come, and the office
confirms whether that works.

The whole process, in one glance:

  1. You book a visit online. This takes a couple of minutes and
     does NOT require you to create an account or install anything.

  2. The office reviews your request and either APPROVES it or
     DECLINES it.

  3. If approved, an email is sent to you with an 8-character
     REFERENCE NUMBER -- your "gate entry code." You also get
     instructions on which gate to use and which ID to bring.

  4. On the day of your visit, you go to the gate, give your
     reference number to the guard, and present the ID you chose
     when booking.

  5. The guard checks your code on the kiosk, the visit is marked
     as completed, and you're allowed in.

You can do all of this from a phone, tablet, or computer. The
website automatically adjusts to whatever screen you're using.


----------------------------------------------------
2. BOOKING A VISIT, STEP BY STEP
----------------------------------------------------

Open the OAS website. The address was given to you, for example a
link on the university's main page or an official email. You'll land
on the booking page.

The booking form is done in TWO STEPS, and a small progress bar at
the top shows where you are.

Before you start, decide which valid ID you will present at the
gate. Examples:

  - A government-issued ID (driver's license, passport, UMID, etc.)
  - Your school ID, if you are a student

You only need to pick WHICH one you will bring -- you do not upload
a photo of it anymore. You simply choose it from a list.


STEP 1 - WHO YOU ARE

Fill in these fields:

  - Full Name
    Your complete name, e.g. "Juan Dela Cruz." This is used to
    identify you in the visit records and to address you in emails.

  - Phone Number
    A number where you can actually be reached, e.g. "0917 123 4567."
    This is your BACKUP CONTACT. If there is ever a problem with
    your appointment and email isn't working (for example, your
    mailbox is full, or an email bounced), the office uses this
    number to reach you.

  - Email Address
    Type this CAREFULLY. All updates about your visit go here,
    and your approval email with your GATE ENTRY CODE is sent to
    this address. A wrong character means you won't get your code.

  - Visitor Category
    Pick who you are from the list: External (Companies, Organizations, Groups), Parents, Student,
    Faculty / Staff, Alumni, or Vendor / Supplier. This helps the
    office understand who is visiting.

  - Valid ID to Present
    Choose from the list the ID you will bring on the day. This is
    the ID the guard will check against you at the gate.

  - Consent Box
    Tick the box to agree that your details may be stored and used
    to process your visit. This is required by Philippine law
    (the Data Privacy Act - RA 10173). Your information is used
    only for your appointment.

When everything is filled in, click "Next: Appointment Details."
If you missed something, the form will point out exactly what needs
fixing -- nothing gets lost.


STEP 2 - YOUR VISIT DETAILS

  1. Pick the office.
     Offices appear as cards. Click the one you want to visit; the
     card you choose gets highlighted.

  2. Pick the date.
     A calendar appears for that office. Each date is marked with a
     colored dot:
       GREEN    - good availability
       AMBER    - nearly full (a few spots left)
       RED      - full for that day (nothing left)
       GREYED   - today already passed, a weekend, or too far ahead
     You can browse months with the arrows. Only MONDAY TO FRIDAY
     can be booked. Click the day you want.

  3. Pick the time.
     Available times for that day are shown as buttons, each labeled
     with how many spots remain. Slots that are FULL or BLOCKED
     (paused by the office) are greyed out. Click a time slot.

  4. Choose how long you'll stay.
     Either 30 minutes (a quick visit) or 60 minutes (an extended
     visit). The office's capacity tells you how many people can
     share the same time slot.

  5. Purpose of visit (optional).
     A short note about why you're visiting, e.g. "Requesting my
     certificate of enrollment." This helps the office prepare.

A BOOKING SUMMARY appears at the bottom, recapping your office,
date, time, and duration. Review it before submitting.


SUBMIT

Click "Submit Appointment." After a moment you'll see a confirmation
screen thanking you and telling you your request is PENDING REVIEW.
A confirmation email is on its way to the address you gave.

If you need to make another request, click "Book Another
Appointment."

NOTE: If two people try to grab the last spot in the same slot at
the same moment, one of you may see "This time slot is fully
booked. Please select another time." Just pick another slot and
try again.


----------------------------------------------------
3. WHAT HAPPENS AFTER YOU BOOK
----------------------------------------------------

Once you submit, one of three things happens:

  - APPROVED. The office accepted your visit. You get an email with
    your 8-character reference number (gate entry code) and the
    gate instructions. Show this at the gate on your appointment
    day.

  - DECLINED. The office couldn't fit you in. You get an email
    telling you so, sometimes with a reason. You are welcome to
    book again another time.

  - STILL PENDING. The office hasn't reviewed it yet. No action is
    needed from you -- just watch your email.

TIP: If you don't see an email right away, check your SPAM or JUNK
folder before asking for help.


----------------------------------------------------
4. THE EMAIL UPDATES YOU'LL RECEIVE
----------------------------------------------------

These are the emails the system can send to you. Each one uses the
email address you gave when booking:

  - Booking confirmation (sent right after you submit)
    Confirms that your request was received and is pending review.
    Includes your appointment details and the office's contact
    information in case you have questions.

  - Approval email (sent when the office says yes)
    The important one. It contains:
        - your appointment details,
        - your REFERENCE NUMBER (your gate entry code), shown large,
        - the ID you need to present, and
        - the gate instructions (USLS Gate 2).
    Keep this email, or write the code down or screenshot it.

  - Decline email (sent when the office says no)
    Lets you know the visit was declined, sometimes with a reason
    and the office's contact details in case you believe it's a
    mistake.

  - Reference re-send (sent when the office issues you a new code)
    Contains a brand-new reference number. Whenever this arrives,
    the LATEST email is the one that works.


----------------------------------------------------
5. ARRIVING ON THE DAY OF YOUR VISIT
----------------------------------------------------

On the day of your appointment:

  1. Go to USLS GATE 2. Entry is accepted only there.
  2. Give the guard your reference number (read it off your phone
     or a printout).
  3. Show the VALID ID you selected when booking. The guard checks
     it against you to make sure the person arriving is the person
     who booked.

A few important things to know about your reference number:

  - It is SINGLE-USE. Once it's entered at the gate, it is used up.
  - It only works on your appointment DATE. It cannot be used on
    another day.
  - If the office ever issues a new code, always use the LATEST one
    you received. Old codes stop working.
  - It can only be used once. If you leave and need to come back the
    same day, the office may issue you a new code.

That's it -- you're ready to visit.


----------------------------------------------------
6. THE INFORMATION YOU SHARE, AND WHY
----------------------------------------------------

When you book, you share a small set of information. Here is what
each piece does:

  Name          Who is visiting. Used to identify you in the visit
                records and to address you politely in emails.

  Phone number  Your BACKUP CONTACT. Used only if the office needs
                to reach you and email isn't possible (bounced
                email, urgent change, etc.).

  Email address The system's main channel to you. All notifications
                are sent here -- including the email that carries
                your GATE ENTRY CODE (reference number).

  Valid ID      The ID you must present at the gate so the guard can
                confirm your identity. Only the type is stored.

  Category      Helps the office understand who is visiting (student,
                faculty, external, parents, etc.).

  Office, date, time, duration
                The schedule of your visit, so the office knows when
                to expect you and can manage capacity.

  Purpose (optional)
                A short note to help the office prepare.

Your information is handled in line with the Data Privacy Act. It is
used only to process your visit, and is kept in the system's secure
databases (see Part C for details).

----------------------------------------------------
7. GETTING INTO THE ADMIN AREA
----------------------------------------------------

The admin area is where staff manage visits. It is separate from the
public booking page and is login-protected, so only authorized staff
can get in.

To log in:

  1. Go to the admin login page (the address given to staff, e.g.
     the /admin link).
  2. Type your EMAIL and PASSWORD.
  3. Click "Sign In." Use the eye icon to show or hide your password
     as you type.

If your email isn't an approved staff account, you'll be told you're
not authorized and returned to the login screen.

FORGOT YOUR PASSWORD? Contact your system manager (the person who
manages staff accounts). They can reset or set up access for you.

When you're done working, click "Sign out" in the sidebar.


----------------------------------------------------
8. THE TWO KINDS OF STAFF ACCOUNTS
----------------------------------------------------

There are two roles:

  - OFFICE STAFF (Office Admin)
    Manages visits for ONE specific office. Sees that office's
    requests and a monthly calendar. This is the role most staff
    will have.

  - SYSTEM MANAGER (Super Admin)
    Has full control: all offices, the full list of visits, office
    setup, staff accounts, and system options. Only a few people
    should have this role.

What you see in the admin area depends on your role. Office staff
get the tools for handling their office's visits; system managers
get extra menu items for offices, accounts, activity logs, and
settings.


----------------------------------------------------
9. THE ADMIN AREA AT A GLANCE
----------------------------------------------------

The admin area has a MENU on the left and the MAIN WORKING AREA in
the middle.

  - On phones and tablets, the menu is tucked behind a "hamburger"
    (three-line) button in the top-left corner. Tap it to open it.
  - Below the menu you'll see who's logged in, your role, and the
    "Sign out" button.

Depending on your role you'll see some or all of these menu items:

  Menu item           | Office staff | System manager
  --------------------+--------------+----------------
  Appointments/Calendar        YES             YES
  Offices                      --              YES
  Accounts                     --              YES
  Activity Log                 --              YES
  Settings                     --              YES


----------------------------------------------------
10. HANDLING BOOKING REQUESTS
----------------------------------------------------

When a visitor books a visit, a new request appears for your office.
Your job is to review it and decide: approve or decline.


REVIEWING A REQUEST

Open the request (click "View" or the person's name). You'll see:

  - The visitor's name, category, email, and phone number.
  - The office, date, and time they picked.
  - The valid ID type they'll present at the gate.
  - Any note they left about the purpose of their visit.

Take a moment to make sure the details are sensible before deciding.


APPROVING A VISIT

If everything looks good, click "Approve." This:

  1. Marks the visit as APPROVED.
  2. Generates a unique 8-character REFERENCE NUMBER for the visitor.
  3. Emails the reference number and gate instructions to the
     visitor's email address.
  4. Records the action in the activity log.

The dashboard updates right away; the email takes a few more seconds
to go out in the background. There is ALSO an "Approve" option
available to you in the email alert you receive -- you can act from
your inbox without opening the dashboard.


TURNING DOWN A VISIT

If you can't fit the visitor in, click "Decline." A small box opens
where you can optionally type a REASON. Click "Confirm Decline."

This marks the visit as declined and emails the visitor -- including
your reason if you gave one. The two-click confirm keeps declines
from being accidental.


VIEWING PAST VISITS

The request list is filtered with tabs at the top:

  Pending | Approved | Declined | History | All

  - The HISTORY tab shows finalized records (declined, completed,
    expired). Open any record to see its full details, including the
    ID the visitor chose to present.

  - The ALL tab shows everything together, and you can search and
    page through the records.


----------------------------------------------------
11. THE MONTHLY CALENDAR VIEW
----------------------------------------------------

Office staff see their office's visits on a MONTHLY CALENDAR -- a
quick visual overview of who is coming and when.

  - Use the ARROWS to move between months.
  - Use the tabs to show only Pending, Approved, or Declined, or
    everything.
  - Each visit appears as a small card with the time and the
    visitor's first name, color-coded:
        AMBER  - pending
        GREEN  - approved
        RED    - declined
  - A day with more than two visits shows "+N more."
  - Today is highlighted so you always know where you are.

Click any visit on the calendar to open its full details, where you
can approve or decline it.


----------------------------------------------------
12. PAUSING TIME SLOTS (BLOCKING)
----------------------------------------------------

Sometimes your office isn't taking visitors -- during lunch, a
meeting, a holiday, or a deadline. Rather than turning people away
after they book, you can PAUSE ("block") specific times ahead of
time.

To block a slot:

  1. Hover over a future day on the calendar. A small "+" appears
     in the corner of the day.
  2. Click it to open the "Block Time Slots" panel for that day.
  3. You'll see all of the day's time slots. CLICK a slot to block
     it -- it turns red.
  4. To re-open it, click the red slot again to unblock it.

Once blocked, that time shows as "Blocked" on the visitor booking
form, so nobody will try to book it.

TIP: Blocking ahead of time is much easier than approving one
visitor and rejecting another who picked the same time.


----------------------------------------------------
13. RE-ISSUING A GATE CODE
----------------------------------------------------

A visitor's reference number is single-use. If a visitor already
entered and needs to come back the same day, or they lost or used
their code, you can issue a NEW reference number:

  1. Open the COMPLETED appointment's details.
  2. Click "Reset QR & Re-send Email."
  3. The visit becomes APPROVED again, a brand-new reference number
     is generated, and an email with the new code goes to the
     visitor.

Use this sparingly -- every use is recorded in the activity log.

14. SETTING UP OFFICES
----------------------------------------------------

> System managers only.

The OFFICES page shows every office that can accept bookings, as
cards showing:

  - Office name and email
  - Operating hours
  - Capacity (how many visitors may share the same time slot)
  - Contact email and phone (what visitors see in their emails for
    follow-up questions)
  - Whether the office is Active or not

ADD AN OFFICE
  1. Click "Add Office."
  2. Enter the office name, email, capacity per time, operating
     hours (e.g. "8:00 AM - 5:00 PM" -- this sets which times
     visitors can pick), contact email and phone, and a short
     description.
  3. Click "Create Office."

EDIT AN OFFICE
  Click "Edit" on a card to change any of its details (including
  the contact email/phone), then "Update Office."

TURN AN OFFICE ON/OFF
  Click "Deactivate" to remove an office from the public booking
  page (existing visits are unaffected). Click "Activate" to bring
  it back.


----------------------------------------------------
15. MANAGING STAFF ACCOUNTS
----------------------------------------------------

> System managers only.

Use the ACCOUNTS page to control who can log in to the admin area.
Each staff account shows the person's name, email, role, and (for
office staff) which office they handle.

ADD A STAFF ACCOUNT
  1. Click "Add Account."
  2. Enter their EMAIL, NAME, and a PASSWORD (at least 6 characters).
  3. Choose their role: Office Staff (manages one office) or System
     Manager (full control).
  4. If office staff, pick the OFFICE they should manage.
  5. Click "Create Account." They can sign in right away.

REMOVE A STAFF ACCOUNT
  1. Click "Remove" on the account.
  2. To confirm, enter YOUR OWN password. This step stops accidental
     removals.
  3. Click "Confirm Remove."


----------------------------------------------------
16. REVIEWING THE ACTIVITY LOG
----------------------------------------------------

> System managers only.

The ACTIVITY LOG is a record of important actions, kept for
accountability. Each line shows:

  - WHEN something happened,
  - WHO did it (their email),
  - WHAT they did (a colored label, e.g., Approve, Decline, Create
    Account, Login),
  - DETAILS -- extra context, such as which person or record was
    involved.

You'll see entries for approving and declining visits, creating and
removing offices and accounts, resetting reference numbers, and
staff logins. If you ever need to check who did what and when, this
is the place.

17. SYSTEM OPTIONS YOU CAN CHANGE
----------------------------------------------------

> System managers only.

The SETTINGS page controls how the whole system behaves. It's split
into simple panels:

SYSTEM INFORMATION
  The system's display name, plus support email and phone numbers.

BOOKING RULES
  - Max advance booking (days): how far ahead visitors can book
    (normally 30).
  - Minimum notice (hours): how far in advance a visit must be

EMAIL NOTIFICATIONS
  Turn each automatic email on or off with a simple switch:
  - Booking confirmation -- to the visitor when they book.
  - Admin alert -- to your office when a new request is pending.
  - Approval email -- to the visitor when accepted (carries the
    reference number).
  - Decline email -- to the visitor when declined.
  - Reference re-send -- when a new code is issued.

OFFICE CONTACT DETAILS
  These are managed per office on the OFFICES page (Section 14), not

When you're done, click "Save Settings" at the bottom.

REMINDER: Email can take a few seconds to send in the background.
If a visitor reports they didn't get a message, double-check that
the matching notification switch here is turned ON.

19. HOW YOUR INFORMATION IS STORED AND PROTECTED
----------------------------------------------------

The data is kept in two places, and both are secured:

  LIVE DATABASE (Supabase)
    This is the "working" copy. Every request, approval, decline,
    and gate entry is written here instantly, and the admin
    dashboard reads from it. It's the system of record. Supabase
    is utilized for real-time faster operations.

  MIRROR DATABASE (cPanel MySQL)
    A backup copy that lives on the university's hosting. Whenever a
    record is created or changed, the same write is also made to
    this backup IN PARALLEL. Its job is resilience: even if one
    database has problems, a full copy of the data still exists in
    the other.

  Who can see the data
    - Visitors can only see what the system emails them about their
      own appointment.
    - Office staff can only see requests for their OWN office.
    - System managers can see everything -- this is deliberate, and
      their actions are themselves logged.

  Protection measures
    - Staff areas are password-protected and require a staff
      account.
    - Roles are enforced on the server for every request -- hiding a
      button in the menu is not the actual protection.
    - Every sensitive action (approve, decline, reset, create/remove
      accounts) is written to the activity log with the actor's
      email.
    - Email action links are tamper-proof and expire (see Section
      20).
    - Passwords are stored hashed, never in plain text.

  Privacy (RA 10173)
    The system collects only the minimum needed to run visit
    scheduling. Visitors consent on the booking form, data is only
    used for its stated purpose, and access is restricted and
    logged. The Data Privacy Act requires exactly this.

  Data retention
    Records stay in the system so the university can answer follow-
    up questions and keep an audit trail. Retention is set and
    reviewed by the system manager. Data collected within the University's
    hosting will be kept for archives, however, data collected through
    Supabase will be deleted every 1st week of the month.


----------------------------------------------------
20. HOW THE GATE CODE WORKS
----------------------------------------------------

Why a code instead of an ID number or a paper form? A reference
number is short enough to read aloud or type quickly, and safe
enough to use as a one-time ticket. Here's how the design works:

  GENERATION
    When a visit is approved, the system generates a random
    8-character code. The alphabet deliberately skips characters
    that are easy to confuse (like 0 and O, or 1 and I), so codes
    are easy to read and hard to mis-type.

  DELIVERY
    The code is emailed to the visitor's email address. Because the
    email carries the code, it is vital that visitors type their
    email correctly -- a wrong character means the code goes
    somewhere else.

  VALIDATION AT THE GATE
    The guard types the code (or the visitor reads it out) into the
    kiosk. The system checks three things:
      1. Does the code exist and is the visit APPROVED?
      2. Is the visit scheduled for TODAY? (Codes don't work on
         other days.)
      3. Has the code NOT been used already? (It is single-use.)

  SINGLE-USE GUARANTEE
    The kiosk only marks the visit completed if it was still
    "approved and unused." If two attempts happen at the same
    moment, only one can win. So a code can't be scanned twice, and
    it can't be replayed later.

  RE-ISSUING
    If a visitor needs to come back the same day, the office resets
    the code. The old code is retired and a NEW one is generated and
    emailed. Only the newest code works.

  EMAIL ACTION LINKS (approve/decline)
    When a new request comes in, office staff receive an email with
    Approve and Decline buttons. These links are protected in two
    ways:
      - They are SIGNED: only this system can create valid links, so
        a link can't be forged or edited.
      - They EXPIRE after 72 hours, so an old email can't be used
        later to change a visit.
    Clicking one acts immediately -- that's why the two-minute email
    review is so fast. A decline from email asks for a reason on the
    page that opens; an approve acts straight away.


----------------------------------------------------
21. THE LIFE OF A VISIT, FROM BOOKING TO ENTRY
----------------------------------------------------

Follow one visit from start to finish:

  1. PENDING - The visitor submits the form. Instantly:
       - the request is saved,
       - a confirmation email goes to the visitor,
       - an alert email goes to the office admin, and
       - a copy is written to the mirror database.

  2. REVIEW - The office admin opens the request and checks it.

  3. Either:
       APPROVED - A reference number is generated and emailed with
                  the gate instructions. Copy mirrored.
       DECLINED - An email with the reason (if given) is sent.
                  Copy mirrored.

  4. GATE ENTRY (only after approval, on the appointment date) -
     The guard enters the code at the kiosk. Validation passes:
       - status becomes COMPLETED,
       - the code is used up (single-use),
       - the kiosk shows the visitor's name, office, time, and the
         ID to ask for,
       - the mirror is updated.

  5. NO-SHOW - If the date passes without entry, the visit becomes
     EXPIRED. Nothing to do -- it lands in History.

Whatever happens, the final status is visible in the History tab,
and the audit trail in the activity log shows who approved or
declined it.


----------------------------------------------------
22. ROLES AND PERMISSIONS IN DETAIL
----------------------------------------------------

The system uses two roles, enforced at the server level:

  OFFICE ADMIN (Office Staff)
    - Sees only their own office's requests, calendar, and history.
    - Can approve, decline, and reset codes for their office.
    - Can block time slots.
    - CANNOT see other offices, manage other staff, change system
      options, or set up offices.

  SYSTEM MANAGER (Super Admin)
    - Everything the office admin can do, PLUS:
    - Sees ALL offices and all appointments.
    - Creates and edits offices (including contact details).
    - Creates and removes staff accounts.
    - Reads the activity log.
    - Changes system options (booking rules, notification switches,
      system name).

  VISITORS (no account)
    - Book appointments.
    - Receive emails (confirmation, approval with code, decline,
      resend).
    - Present their code at the gate.
    - Cannot log into the admin area at all.

Why does this matter? It keeps mistakes and privacy problems small:
an office admin simply has no way to reach another office's records,
even by accident.

23. WHAT EVERY STATUS MEANS
----------------------------------------------------

  STATUS      WHAT IT MEANS            WHAT HAPPENS NEXT
  ----------  ------------------------  --------------------------
  Pending     Booked, waiting for      Office approves or
              your decision            declines it
  Approved    You accepted it          Visitor gets their code by
                                       email
  Declined    You turned it down       Visitor is emailed (with
                                       your reason, if given)
  Completed   Visitor entered the      You can re-issue a new
              gate (code was used)     code if needed
  Expired     The visit date passed    Nothing to do
              without being used

Remember: a visitor approved a week ago with a code already used at
the gate shows COMPLETED -- that is normal. Only an UNUSED approved
code is enterable at the gate.

24. HOW THE SYSTEM IS BUILT
----------------------------------------------------

The following details are those used during development:

  - Build: Next.js application served on Vercel.
  - Live data: Supabase (PostgreSQL). Tables include appointments,
    offices, admins, settings, and email logs.
  - Backup data: a single cPanel MySQL appointments table, mirrored
    on every write (create, approve, decline, gate entry, reset).
    Mirror failures are logged and never fail the visitor's request.
  - Email: transactional email API (Resend/SMTP). Emails are sent
    in the background so booking stays fast.
  - Codes: server-generated 8-character reference numbers stored in
    the appointment record (qr_token column, kept for historical
    naming). Single-use is enforced by an atomic conditional update
    at the gate.
  - Email action links: HMAC-signed approve/decline URLs with a
    72-hour expiry, so a link can't be forged or replayed later.
  - Security: role-based access control is enforced server-side in
    every API route; passwords are hashed; audit entries record
    every privileged action.
  - Public flow: booking page (two-step form) -> user emails ->
    admin dashboard (approve/decline) -> visitor email with code ->
    gate kiosk (enter code) -> completed.

Developer notes and the full technical specification live in the
project's PROMPT.md and PROGRAM_FLOW.txt files.

25. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
----------------------------------------------------

Q: Do visitors need their own account to book?
A: No. Booking works without an account -- just fill in the form.

Q: How far ahead can someone book?
A: Usually from today up to 30 days in advance (the system manager
   can change this). Weekends cannot be booked.

Q: Can someone book more than one spot at the same time?
A: Each form is for one person. For a group, book separately. Each
   office limits how many people may share the same time slot.

Q: A visitor's gate code isn't working.
A: Check it's the LATEST code, they're on the CORRECT day, and it
   hasn't ALREADY been used. If it was used by mistake, reset the
   code and re-send it (Section 13).

Q: A visitor says they didn't get an email.
A: Ask them to check spam/junk first. Then confirm their email
   address is correct, and that the matching notification switch in
   Settings (Section 17) is turned on.

Q: Can a visit be rescheduled?
A: Not directly. For approved or expired visits, decline and let the
   visitor rebook. For completed visits, issue a new code with the
   reset option.

Q: What if a visitor doesn't show up?
A: Nothing to do. The visit shows as EXPIRED once the date passes
   and lands in History.

Q: Can office staff see other offices' visits?
A: No -- office staff only see their own office. System managers see
   everything.

Q: Why do we ask for a phone number if everything is by email?
A: The phone number is the BACKUP contact. If the office can't reach
   a visitor by email (a bounced address, an urgent change, a filled
   mailbox), the phone is the alternative. It is never used for
   anything else.

Q: Why does the email field matter so much?
A: Because the email carries the GATE ENTRY CODE. If it's typed
   wrong, the visitor's code goes to the wrong inbox and they can't
   get in. Help visitors double-check it.

Q: What information is collected and why?
A: Name (identification), phone (backup contact), email (all
   notifications, including the gate code), the type of valid ID
   they'll present (gate identity check), category, office/date/time,
   and an optional purpose note. All handled in line with the Data
   Privacy Act; visitors consent on the booking form.

Q: Who do we contact for help?
A: Visitors use the office contact details shown in their emails.
   Staff go to their system manager for access, resets, or settings
   help.

26. COMMON PROBLEMS AND HOW TO SOLVE THEM
----------------------------------------------------

PROBLEM: A visitor booked, but no confirmation email arrived.
  Check: spam/junk folder -> correct email address -> the Booking
  Confirmation switch in Settings is ON -> the visitor's details
  exist in Pending.

PROBLEM: A visitor is at the gate and their code won't work.
  Check the three rules: latest code? correct day? already used?
  If already used, reset and issue a new code (Section 13).

PROBLEM: Office staff can't see a request.
  They may be assigned to the wrong office. Have the system manager
  check the Accounts page (Section 15) and the office assignment.

PROBLEM: A time slot that should be available shows as Blocked.
  Open the calendar for that day and click the red slot to unblock
  it (Section 12).

PROBLEM: Someone says the system is slow or the email is late.
  Emails are sent in the background and can take a few seconds.
  If a page really hangs, log out and back in, and try again after
  a moment.

PROBLEM: Forgot the admin password.
  The system manager can create a new account or re-issue access
  from the Accounts page.
