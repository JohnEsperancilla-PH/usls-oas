# USLS Online Appointment System (OAS) — User Manual

Welcome to the USLS OAS user manual. This book shows everyone who uses the system — from visitors booking a visit to staff running an office — exactly how to do their part. It is written in plain language with step-by-step instructions, so you can follow along from start to finish.

This manual has two main parts:

- **Part A — For Visitors:** how to book a visit, what happens next, and how to use your QR code to enter.
- **Part B — For Staff (the Admin area):** how to log in, check booking requests, approve or turn down visits, and run your office's schedule.

Use the table of contents to jump to whatever you need.

# Part A — For Visitors

## 1. What the system does for you

The Online Appointment System lets you book a visit to a university office ahead of time — no waiting in line, no walking in and hoping for the best.

Here is the whole process in one glance:

1. You **book a visit** online. This takes just a couple of minutes and does **not** require you to create an account.
2. The office **reviews** your request and either **accepts** or **declines** it.
3. If accepted, you get an email with a **QR code**.
4. On the day of your visit, you **show the QR code** at the gate.
5. Gate staff scan it, compare your **ID picture** to you, and let you in.

You can do all of this from a **phone, tablet, or computer.** The website adjusts to whatever screen you're using.

---

## 2. Booking a visit, step by step

Open the OAS website (the address was given to you — for example the university appointment page or link). You'll land on the **booking page**.

The booking form is done in **two steps**, shown as a small progress bar at the top.

> **Before you start:** have a clear **photo of your ID** ready on your phone or computer. This can be a government-issued ID (driver's license, passport) or your **school ID** if you're a student. Clear JPEG or PNG photos work best.

### Step 1 — Who you are

Fill in these fields:

- **Full Name** — your complete name (e.g., *Juan Dela Cruz*).
- **Phone Number** — a number where you can be reached (e.g., *0917 123 4567*).
- **Email Address** — type this **carefully**, because all updates about your visit are sent here.
- **Visitor Category** — pick who you are from the drop-down:
  - General Public
  - Student
  - Faculty / Staff
  - Alumni
  - Vendor / Supplier
- **Upload your ID** — upload the photo of your ID. Either:
  - **click** the large dashed box, or
  - **drag and drop** the picture onto it.
  
  The system resizes the picture automatically so it uploads quickly. You'll see short messages like *"Compressing image..."* and *"Uploading image..."* — just wait for those to finish. Once it's done you'll see a small preview of your ID. (There's an "×" on the corner if you want to remove it and try again.)

> **Note:** You won't be able to continue until the ID picture has finished uploading.

- **Consent box** — tick the box to agree to your ID picture and details being kept for verification. This is required by law (the Data Privacy Act). Your information is only used to process your visit.

When everything is filled in, click **"Next: Appointment Details."** If you missed something, the form will point out exactly what needs fixing.

### Step 2 — Your visit details

**1. Pick the office.** A list of offices appears as cards. Click the office you want to visit. The card you choose gets highlighted in green.

**2. Pick the date.** A calendar appears for that office. Each date is marked with a colored dot:
- **Green** — good availability
- **Amber** — nearly full (a few spots left)
- **Red** — full for that day
- **Greyed out** — can't choose (today already passed, a weekend, or too far ahead)

Use the arrows to flip months. Only **Monday to Friday** can be booked. Click the day you want.

**3. Pick the time.** The available times for that day are shown as buttons. Each shows how many spots are left. Slots that are **Full** or **Blocked** (paused by the office) are greyed out. Click a time you like.

**4. Choose how long you'll stay.** Pick either:
- **30 minutes** — a quick visit, or
- **60 minutes** — an extended visit.

**5. Purpose of visit *(optional)*.** You can type in a short note about why you're visiting. This is optional.

When you've made your choices, a **Booking Summary** shows at the bottom recapping your office, date, time, and how long you'll stay.

### Submit

Click **"Submit Appointment."** After a moment you'll see a confirmation screen thanking you and telling you your request is **pending review**. A confirmation email is on its way to the address you gave.

You can click **"Book Another Appointment"** if you need to make another request.

> **Just a heads-up:** If two people try to grab the last spot at the same moment, you may see a message like *"This time slot is fully booked. Please select another time."* Just pick another time and try again.

---

## 3. What happens after you book

Once you submit, three outcomes are possible:

- **Your visit is accepted.** The office approved it. You'll get an email with a **QR code** attached. You'll show this at the gate (see next section).
- **Your visit is turned down.** The office couldn't fit you in. You'll get an email letting you know, sometimes with a reason and the office's contact details. You can book again anytime.
- **Still waiting.** The office hasn't reviewed it yet. No action needed from you — just watch your email.

> **Tip:** Check your **spam or junk** folder if you don't see the email right away.

---

## 4. Arriving on the day of your visit

On the day of your visit, bring the **QR code** you got by email. You can show it on your phone screen or print it.

A few important things to know:

- The QR code works for **one entry only** — it's used up after it's scanned once.
- It only works on **your appointment date**. It can't be used on a different day.
- If the office ever re-issues your QR code, they'll email you a **new one**. Always use the **latest** code you received.
- Bring the **ID you uploaded** too — gate staff will compare it with your picture to make sure the person arriving is who booked.

That's it — you're ready to visit. 🎉

# Part B — For Staff

## 5. Getting into the admin area

The admin area is where staff manage visits. It's separate from the public booking page and is **login-protected**, so only authorized staff can get in.

**To log in:**
1. Go to the admin login page (the address for staff, e.g. `/admin`).
2. Type your **email** and **password**.
3. Click **"Sign In."** There's an eye icon to show/hide your password as you type.

If your email isn't an approved staff account, you'll be told you're not authorized, and you'll be returned to the login screen.

> **Forgot your password?** Contact your system administrator (the person who manages staff accounts). They can set up access for you — see [Section 11](#11-managing-staff-accounts-advanced).

When you're done working, click **"Sign out"** in the sidebar.

### Two kinds of staff accounts

- **Office staff (Office Admin)** — manages visits for **one specific office** and sees a **monthly calendar**. This is the role most staff members will have.
- **System manager (Super Admin)** — has full control: all offices, a full list of visits, staff accounts, and system options. Only a few people should have this.

What you see in the admin area depends on your role. Office staff get the calendar and handling tools; system managers get extra menu items for offices, accounts, activity logs, and settings.

## 6. The admin area at a glance

The admin area has a **menu on the left** and the **main working area** in the middle.

- On phones/tablets, the menu is tucked behind a **☰ button** in the top-left corner. Tap it to open the menu.
- Below the menu you'll see **who's logged in** and your role, plus the **Sign out** button.

Depending on your role you'll see some or all of these menu items:

| Menu item | Office staff | System manager |
|---|---|---|
| Appointments / Calendar | ✓ | ✓ |
| Offices | — | ✓ |
| Accounts | — | ✓ |
| Activity Log | — | ✓ |
| Settings | — | ✓ |

## 7. Handling booking requests

When a visitor books a visit, a new request appears in the admin area for your office. Your job is to review it and decide.

### Reviewing a request

Open the request (click **View** or the person's name). You'll see:
- The visitor's name, category (student, general public, etc.), email, and phone number.
- The office, date, and time they picked.
- Any note they left about the purpose of their visit.
- A **picture of their ID**, which you should check.

### Accepting a visit

If everything looks good, click **"Approve."** This:
1. Marks the visit as **approved**.
2. Creates a **QR code** for the visitor.
3. Emails the **QR code** to them.
4. Adds the visit to the office calendar (if set up) and notes it in the activity log.

The dashboard responds right away, even though the email takes a few more seconds to go out in the background.

### Turning down a visit

If you can't fit the visitor in, click **"Decline."** A small box appears where you can (optionally) type a **reason**. Click **"Confirm Decline."**

This marks the visit as declined and emails the visitor to let them know (including your reason if you gave one).

### Re-issuing a QR code

If a visitor already entered and needs to come back again the same day (or they lost/used their code), you can issue a **new** QR code:
- Open the **completed** appointment's details.
- Click **"Reset QR & Re-send Email."**
- This marks it approved again, makes a **brand-new QR code**, and emails it to the visitor.

> Use this sparingly — each use is recorded in the activity log.

### Viewing past / saved visits

The list of requests can be filtered using the tabs at the top: **Pending, Approved, Declined, History, All.**

- The **History** tab shows finalized records (declined, completed, expired). Open any record to see its full details, including the valid ID presented at the gate.

---

## 8. The monthly calendar view

Office staff see their office's visits on a **monthly calendar** — a quick visual overview of who's coming and when.

- Use the **arrows** to move between months.
- Use the **tabs** to show only Pending, Approved, or Declined, or everything.
- Each visit appears as a small card with the **time** and the **visitor's first name**, color-coded:
  - **Amber** — pending
  - **Green** — approved
  - **Red** — declined
- A day with more than two visits shows **"+N more."**
- **Today** is highlighted so you always know the date.

Click any visit on the calendar to open its full details, where you can **Approve** or **Decline** it (see [Section 7](#7-handling-booking-requests)).

---

## 9. Pausing time slots (blocking)

Sometimes your office isn't taking visitors — during lunch, a meeting, a holiday, or a deadline. Rather than turning people away after they book, you can **pause** ("block") specific times ahead of time.

**How to block a slot:**
1. Hover over a future day on the calendar — a small **"+"** appears in the corner of the day.
2. Click it to open the **Block Time Slots** panel for that day.
3. You'll see all the day's time slots. **Click a slot to block it** — it turns red.
4. To re-open it, **click the red slot again** to unblock it.

Once blocked, that time is hidden as "Blocked" in the visitor booking form, so nobody will try to book it.

> Blocking ahead of time is much easier than approving one visitor and rejecting another who picked the same time.

## 10. Setting up offices (Advanced)

> **System managers only.** This section is for the people who maintain the system's list of offices.

The **Offices** page shows every office that can accept bookings, shown as cards with the office name, email, **operating hours**, **capacity** (how many visitors can share the same time), **contact email/phone** (shown to visitors for follow-ups), and whether it's **Active**.

**Add an office**
1. Click **"Add Office."**
2. Enter the office **name**, **email**, **capacity per time** (how many visitors per slot), **operating hours** (e.g., *8:00 AM - 5:00 PM* — this sets which times visitors can pick), **contact email/phone** (what visitors see in their emails for follow-ups), and a short **description**.
3. Click **"Create Office."**

**Edit an office** — click **"Edit"** on a card to change its details (including contact email/phone), then **"Update Office."**

**Turn an office on/off** — click **"Deactivate"** to remove an office from the public booking page (existing visits are unaffected), or **"Activate"** to bring it back.

---

## 11. Managing staff accounts (Advanced)

> **System managers only.** Use the **Accounts** page to control who can log in to the admin area.

Each staff account shows the person's name, email, **role**, and (for office staff) which office they handle.

**Add a staff account**
1. Click **"Add Account."**
2. Enter their **email**, **name**, and a **password** (at least 6 characters).
3. Choose their **role**:
   - **Office Staff** — manages one office, or
   - **System Manager** — full control.
4. If **office staff**, pick the **office** they should manage.
5. Click **"Create Account."** They can sign in right away with these details.

**Remove a staff account**
1. Click **"Remove"** on the account.
2. To confirm, enter **your own password**. This step is there to stop accidental deletions.
3. Click **"Confirm Remove."**

---

## 12. Reviewing the activity log (Advanced)

> **System managers only.** The **Activity Log** is a record of important actions for accountability.

It lists, line by line:
- **When** something happened
- **Who** did it (their email)
- **What** they did (a colored label, e.g. Approve, Decline, Create Account, Login)
- **Details** — extra info like which person/record it involved

You'll see entries for approving and declining visits, creating and removing offices and accounts, and staff logins. If you ever need to check who did what and when, this is where to look.

---

## 13. System options you can change (Advanced)

> **System managers only.** The **Settings** page controls how the whole system behaves. It's split into a few simple panels.

**System information** — the system's display name, plus support email/phone numbers.

**Booking rules**
- **Max advance booking (days)** — how far ahead visitors can book (normally 30).
- **Minimum notice (hours)** — how far in advance a visit must be booked.

**Email notifications** — turn each automatic email on or off with a simple switch:
- **Booking confirmation** — to the visitor when they book.
- **Admin alert** — to your office when a new request is pending.
- **Approval email** — to the visitor when accepted (carries the reference number).
- **Decline email** — to the visitor when declined.
- **Reference re-send** — when a reference number is re-issued.

> **Office contact details** (the email/phone shown to visitors) are managed per office on the **Offices** page — see [Section 10](#10-setting-up-offices-advanced).

When you're done, click **"Save Settings"** at the bottom.

> **Reminder:** Email can take a few seconds to send in the background, so if a visitor reports they haven't gotten a message, double-check that the matching notification switch here is turned on.

# Reference

## 14. What each booking status means

| Status | What it means | What happens next |
|---|---|---|
| **Pending** | The visitor booked; waiting for your decision | You accept or decline it |
| **Approved** | You accepted it | Visitor gets their QR code by email |
| **Declined** | You turned it down | Visitor is emailed (with a reason, if you gave one) |
| **Completed** | The visitor entered (QR was scanned at the gate) | You can re-issue a QR if needed |
| **Expired** | The visit date passed without being used | Nothing to do |

## 15. Questions and answers

**Q: Do visitors need their own account to book?**
No. Booking works without an account — just fill in the form.

**Q: How far ahead can someone book?**
Usually from today up to 30 days in advance (the system manager can change this). Weekends can't be booked.

**Q: Can someone book more than one spot at the same time?**
Each form is for one person. For a group, book separately. Each office limits how many people can share the same time.

**Q: A visitor's QR code isn't working.**
Check it's the **latest** code, they're on the **correct day**, and it hasn't **already been scanned**. If it was used by mistake, reset the QR and re-send it (see [Section 7](#7-handling-booking-requests)).

**Q: A visitor says they didn't get an email.**
Ask them to check spam/junk. Confirm the notification switches are on in Settings, and the email address they gave is correct.

**Q: Can a visit be rescheduled?**
Not directly. For approved/expired visits, you can either decline-and-let-them-rebook, or for completed visits re-issue a QR with the **Reset QR** option.

**Q: What if a visitor doesn't show up?**
Nothing needs to be done. The visit will show as **expired** once the date passes and can then be archived.

**Q: Can office staff see other offices' visits?**
No — office staff only see their **own office**. System managers see everything.

**Q: What information is collected and why?**
Name, contact details, visitor category, purpose of visit, and an ID picture. This is used solely to verify and process visits, and is handled in line with the **Data Privacy Act**. Visitors consent to this on the booking form.

**Q: Who do we contact for help?**
Visitors can use the office contact details shown in their emails. Staff should go to their **system manager** for access, resets, or settings help.
